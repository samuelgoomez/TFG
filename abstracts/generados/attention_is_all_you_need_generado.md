This study presents the Transformer, an innovative architecture designed to overcome the limitations of recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and seq2seq models in language processing, specifically in machine translation. By replacing recurrent layers with attention mechanisms, the Transformer facilitates improved parallelization and enhances translation quality. Employing a multi-head attention mechanism, the model establishes connections between all input and output words, producing remarkable outcomes. The Transformer achieves BLEU scores of 28.4 for English-to-German translation and 41.0 for English-to-French, setting new state-of-the-art benchmarks for both tasks. These findings indicate that the Transformer not only enhances translation quality compared to traditional architectures but also trains more efficiently, highlighting its potential for broader applications in tasks that include text input and output.
